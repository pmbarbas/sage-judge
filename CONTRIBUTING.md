# Contributing

Propose focused changes using issues or pull requests. Include a synthetic reproducer, expected outcome, actual outcome, relevant contract version and regression tests. Avoid customer data, secrets, private documents, third-party code without clear rights or new claims of semantic accuracy. Keep evidence admission, formal reasoning, replay and policy distinct. Do not weaken a fail-closed test to obtain a favourable result.

By submitting a contribution for inclusion, confirm that you have the right to submit it under the project's MIT licence. Preserve applicable notices. Do not submit confidential or employer-owned material without permission. Maintainers may decline contributions outside the bounded scope.
